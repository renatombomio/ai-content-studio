# Cocoa Talk — Editorial Bible

---

## Mission

Cocoa Talk exists to create space for people to slow down, reflect, and reconnect with themselves.

Not inspiration. Not motivation. Not self-help.

A quiet moment in a world that moves too fast.

---

## Vision

To become the most recognisable introspective content brand in Spanish-speaking social media.

People should recognise Cocoa Talk without reading the account name — through the typography, the mascot, the writing, the pacing. Everything unmistakably Cocoa Talk.

---

## Audience

People who feel things deeply but rarely say so out loud.

People who are somewhere in the middle of a quiet personal journey — not in crisis, not triumphant, just honest.

Age: 20–38.
Language: Spanish.
Platform: TikTok.

They are not looking for advice. They are looking for recognition.

---

## Editorial Pillars

Every piece of content belongs to one of four pillars.

---

### 1. Shadow Work

Deep introspective reflections.

Explores what people carry without naming it: emotional wounds, self-awareness, childhood, the inner child, fear, shame, boundaries, healing, acceptance.

Not clinical. Not therapeutic. Human.

The writing names what the reader already feels.

---

### 2. Poetic Writing

Original writing only. Never imitation. Never adaptation of existing poems.

Short. Minimal. Quiet. Emotionally precise.

A Cocoa Talk poem is not decorated. It is stripped until only what is true remains.

The reader should feel understood, not impressed.

---

### 3. Intrapersonal Relationships

The relationship people have with themselves.

Self-talk. Loneliness. Identity. Growth. Self-respect. Forgiveness. Acceptance.

This pillar is about the interior — what happens inside before it shows outside.

The writing is close. Like someone who knows you better than you've let on.

---

### 4. Mental Health Reflections

Human reflections on psychological experience. Not therapy. Not medical advice.

Calm. Empathetic. Gentle.

The writing does not diagnose or prescribe. It witnesses.

---

## Voice

The Cocoa Talk voice has one register: quiet and close.

Like someone speaking in a low voice in a warm room. Not performing. Not broadcasting.

**Always:**
- Second person when universal: *"A veces te preguntas si alguien nota cuando estás bien."*
- Third person when observing: *"Ella guardó los mensajes aunque le dolían leer."*
- Fragments when silence carries more than words.

**Never:**
- Motivational language.
- Imperatives that instruct the reader.
- Sentences that could appear on an airport poster.
- Sentences that name the emotion instead of evoking it.
- Advice.
- Explanations.
- Moral conclusions.

If a sentence is trying to impress the reader, delete it.

---

## Writing Philosophy

Every piece has one emotional truth. Not a lesson. Not a message. A feeling.

Start with something specific. A detail. A moment. An image.

Let the particular become universal.

End before the resolution. The reader completes it.

The writing earns its ending without announcing it.

## Visual Identity

**Color Palette:**

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Near-black brown | `#1C0F07` | Backgrounds, shadows |
| Dark red | `#6B1A1A` | Accent, borders |
| Burgundy | `#8B2E2E` | Secondary accent |
| Warm red | `#A03030` | Highlights |
| Tan / caramel | `#C8956B` | Mid-tones |
| Cream | `#E8D9B5` | Typography, light areas |
| Teal green | `#2E6B5E` | Mug, brand accent |

**Visual Language:**

Autumn leaves. Rain on glass. Bookshelves. Soft lamps. Wood surfaces. Fireplace glow. Notebooks. Warm amber light. Bokeh backgrounds.

Never: bright social-media aesthetics. Never: flashy transitions. Never: high-contrast pop colours.

---

## Typography

Warm. Serif. Readable. Timeless.

Feels like a book you want to keep.

Typography is not decoration. It is the story.

In video: subtitles are the primary storytelling element. There is no voice-over. The text appears on screen while the footage plays.

---

## Mascot

The mascot is Coco. See `docs/COCO.md` for the full character reference.

Coco is the warm presence behind every Cocoa Talk moment. Not a cartoon character. Not a spokesperson. A host.

Coco appears in:
- The Cocoa Question of the Week (carousel)
- Brand identity slides
- Future mascot-led formats

Coco never appears in the video footage pipeline.

---

## Content Formats

### Automated Video

Vertical (9:16). Duration: 30–90 seconds.

```
Idea → Brain → Writing → Assets → Typography → FFmpeg → TikTok
```

Music is selected inside TikTok. The pipeline produces no audio.
The writing appears as animated subtitles over cinematic footage.

Published twice per week.

---

### Cocoa Question of the Week

Two-slide carousel. Published once per week.

**Slide 1:** Brand identity card. Coco mascot. "Cocoa Question of the Week."

**Slide 2:** One powerful reflective question. Minimal typography. No distractions.

---

## Things Cocoa Talk Never Does

- Generate voice-over narration with robotic TTS.
- Publish content that sounds like advice.
- Use hashtags like #fyp or #viral.
- Post anything that feels performative.
- Rush the pacing.
- Explain the emotion.
- Name the feeling instead of evoking it.
- Imitate other creators or existing written works.
- Use bright, flashy, or hyperactive visual styles.
- Appear loud.

---

## The Standard

Before any piece of content is published, ask one question:

*Does this feel true?*

Not clever. Not profound. Not optimised.

True.
